title: Tighter fights and smarter CPU rivals
date: 2026-11-16
build: alpha 6 rc2
summary: Haste lasts through a whole fight, the lightning trap is toned down, and CPU rivals learn to keep a home guard.
---

A smaller build focused on balance and on making the CPU rivals behave more sensibly.

## Creatures and powers

- The haste spell now lasts long enough to carry a creature through a whole fight.
- A creature in the middle of teleporting can no longer be grabbed with your hand.
- In a match with three or four lairs, every extra rival now gets its own funds and buys its own diggers.

## Traps and rooms

- The lightning trap now hits for 6 instead of 14, so a hero party takes more shots to bring down.
- Hovering your treasury with the hand shows how much the next grab takes ("Grab 250 gold").
- A squad leader in the barracks gets a small "S" badge.
- Every beat of a cave-in now flashes and throws rubble.

## Levels

- The first bonus level now starts with 500 gold, because powers and new diggers cost gold and the level has none to mine.
- Level scripts can write their own objectives and pop-ups.
- Every kind of notice has its own icon.

## CPU rivals

- The CPU keeps a little gold back before buying a digger, so it can still pay new arrivals.
- On levels where heroes attack while its army is away, the CPU now keeps a few fighters at home. That wins several of the harder levels much more often.
