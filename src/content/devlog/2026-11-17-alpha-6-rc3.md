A short build, but it lays the groundwork for multiplayer.

## Creatures and powers

- Rallying creatures to your heart works from anywhere on the map again.
- A thief that grows up enough becomes a knight.
- A creature swapped by a level script now saves and loads correctly.

## Screen and hints

- Messages from the level script now show as quick text on the HUD.

## Levels

- Winning an extra level now opens the next two.
- One campaign level no longer stalls when the last few heroes wait at their gate after the rival falls.

## Multiplayer groundwork

- In a match with a third or fourth lair, each extra lair now has its own heart and is knocked out when it falls. This is behind a setting for now.
