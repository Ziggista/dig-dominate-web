---
title: Powers now cost gold
date: 2026-11-15
build: alpha 6 rc1
summary: The mana bar is gone. Every power is paid for from your treasury, creatures learn attacks as they level, and the CPU rival raids at a fairer pace.
---

This was a big build. The headline change is that powers now come out of the same gold pile as your rooms, so you are always choosing between building and casting.

## Your powers

- Casting a power costs gold from your treasury. There is no mana bar any more.
- Powers you can charge up by holding the button cost one, two or three times their base price. If you cannot afford a cast, it simply does not happen.
- Creating a new digger costs 30 gold, a heal 60 and a lightning strike 120.

## Creatures grow up

- Creatures now learn their attacks and buffs at set experience levels, so a young spellcaster fights very differently from a veteran one.
- Heroes level up the same way, so a high-level hero party is a real threat.
- Every hit teaches the attacker something, and tougher targets teach more.
- Some creatures gain experience while they sleep, if their lair sits next to the terrain they like.
- Buffs and curses last much longer than before, and longer still on high-level creatures.
- An enemy within three tiles can now spot an invisible creature.

## Traps, doors and the workshop

- Several traps now fire a burst of shots with a pause between each, so one trap can wear down a whole group.
- Your workshop has to be bigger before it can make the strongest traps and doors.
- Doors now stand where the level maps put them, and only their owner can walk through.

## Fights and orders

- Select creatures and press **U** to have them guard the spot under your cursor. They hold it, fight anything that comes near, and return afterwards.
- Right-clicking a rival's heart sends your selected creatures marching to attack it.
- A fight far from home now only calls the creatures that can actually see it. A fight at your heart still calls everyone.
- Diggers now take 9 seconds to convert an enemy tile, so a rival eats into your land more slowly.

## Maps and levels

- Rock, gold and gem seams are shown from the start. Enemy land and rooms stay hidden until you find them.
- Enemy creatures stay visible on ground you have already explored.
- Special boxes now lie on the map and trigger when one of your creatures walks onto them.
- The bonus levels have their lava and water back.

## The CPU rival

- The CPU rival now waits about five minutes between raids instead of sending a new party every half minute, which makes the hardest mid-campaign level beatable.
- It trains its strongest creatures first.

## Saving and replays

- **Main menu > Replays > Watch** plays a saved replay back in the game view. Press **P** to pause, **+** and **-** for speed, and **,** and **.** to skip 30 seconds.
- Several situations where a saved game would fail to load are fixed, including saving in the middle of a fight.
